Candidate Advisory

Examination Protocol & Code of Conduct

Ensure complete compliance with our strict academic integrity standards before entering the testing environment.

Mandatory Instructions (DO's)
  • Sit in a well-lit room with your face clearly visible to the camera at all times.
  • Have your original College/National Photo ID Card ready for identity verification.
  • Ensure your device is connected to a power supply or has at least 80% battery backup.
  • Use the latest versions of Google Chrome, MS Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
Strictly Prohibited (DON'Ts)
  • Do not switch browser tabs or minimize the examination window (3 warnings will terminate test).
  • Smartphones, smartwatches, external earbuds, and secondary monitors are strictly forbidden.
  • No other person is permitted to enter the testing area during the live session.
  • Do not read questions aloud; unexpected background speech is flagged by the audio auditor.
